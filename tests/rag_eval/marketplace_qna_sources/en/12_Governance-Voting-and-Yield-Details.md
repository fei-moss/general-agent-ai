## Governance Voting and Yield Details

This `ballot` document covers proposal-page outcomes, Governance-versus-Trading share-price behavior, and project launch value. Core holder mechanics live in Governance Agent (Ballot); project, token, and fixed APY values must come from current Agent context.

**Q: How is my voting power calculated?**

From a snapshot of your holdings taken when the proposal went live, not from what you hold at the moment you vote.

**Q: A proposal is already open. Can I mint now and vote on it?**

No. The snapshot locks in when the proposal is created, and any later change to your holdings has no effect on that proposal.

**Q: The page shows 0 votes and says the snapshot recorded no voting power for my wallet. What does that mean?**

Your shares arrived after the snapshot. Compare the proposal-page record with when you received them.

**Q: Can someone buy a large number of shares at the last minute and sway the outcome?**

No. Voting power comes from the snapshot taken at proposal creation, so shares acquired after a proposal goes live carry no voting power.

**Q: What voting options does a proposal have?**

For, Against, and Abstain. Current totals appear on the proposal page, and past proposals appear under Proposal History.

**Q: Does voting cost gas?**

Yes. Voting happens on chain and carries the standard network fee for whichever network the Agent runs on.

**Q: Where do returns on a Governance Agent come from?**

Shares accrue at a fixed APY, and the project pays the rewards in its own token. Read the actual values from current Agent context.

**Q: When do the rewards arrive?**

You claim them together with your redemption. Nothing pays out while you hold.

**Q: Is redemption the same as on a Trading Agent?**

The procedure matches a Trading Agent: submit Request Redeem, then use View history. Governance holder rules remain in Governance Agent (Ballot) and current Agent context.

**Q: Product comparison: will a Governance Agent's share price move up and down like a Trading Agent's?**

A Governance Agent share price does not move up and down with trading performance like a Trading Agent share price. It accrues at a fixed APY; a Trading Agent share price follows managed assets.

**Q: We want to launch a Governance Agent. How do we apply?**

Moss currently deploys Governance Agents for projects on a custom basis rather than through self-service. Contact the Moss Telegram or Discord community.

**Q: How does a project benefit from launching a Governance Agent?**

It turns token holders into participants you can reach and who can vote. Holders can Mint shares, receive the project's disclosed rewards, and vote on on-chain proposals.
