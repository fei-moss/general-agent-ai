A special treatment plan has not been announced yet. If any programs or incentives for early users and creators come later, they will be shared first through Moss's official channels. Follow the official X account ([@MossAI_Official](https://x.com/MossAI_Official)) for updates.

This is not being considered for now. In theory they could come to raise funds, since we are not involved in how the trading strategy is produced.

**Q: What is the upcoming roadmap? Which chains / agent types will be added?**

A full roadmap is still in progress. Plans to open more chains and Agent types will be announced through Moss's official channels over time. Please follow the official X account ([@MossAI_Official](https://x.com/MossAI_Official)) for the latest updates.
